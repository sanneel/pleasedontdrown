# Cast designs: the GPT prompts (2026-10-20)

Concept pictures for every character, drawn on the two chosen bases (`New-Game-2026-10-20/base_woman.png`,
`base_man.png`). They show the textures, hair, hats and body sliders to make; they are **not** turned into new
3D bodies (see `21-Base-Body-Prompts.md`).

## How to run one

Attach, every time: the matching base (`base_woman.png` or `base_man.png`) and the two style pictures
(`style_reference_clay_tourists.webp`, `style_reference_bikini_girls.png`). Paste the **template**, then put the
character's paragraph in place of `<WHO>`. One character per chat (or start a new image each time), so they don't
drift into each other.

### The template

```
The attached base body, exactly the same body, A-pose, proportions, head size, hands, feet and camera, drawn in the
attached art style (soft matte clay-toy 3D cartoon). An adult, never childlike.

<WHO>

Only change: skin colour, hair, face details (eyebrows, eye colour, nose shape, wrinkles, freckles, moustache,
beard, makeup), clothes, shoes and small accessories, and the body build only where it says so. Keep the A-pose
with the arms and legs apart. Nothing held in the hands. Hats, hair and glasses sit naturally on the head.

Character turnaround sheet: front, three-quarter, side, back, side by side, same scale, on the same line. Plain
light grey background, even soft light, no floor shadow, no text.
```

## The players

**Goofy, the lifeguard** (base: man). Use the P3 prompt in `21-Base-Body-Prompts.md` (it also needs
`goofy_current_turnaround.png` attached), or this paragraph in the template:

Goofy, the players' lifeguard, about 30, the silliest-looking man on the beach. His gag, keep it big: huge round
googly eyes (white, small black pupils, bulging out of the face), two big buck teeth in a wide happy grin, a big

## The pirates (Skull Cove, island 3)

No hooks, no peg legs, no weapons in the hands (they'd break the shared skeleton and the grips): the gags go in the
faces, the hats and the clothes. Weapons stay as the game's own items.

```
The Pirate King (base: man, a much bigger round belly, a little taller), about 60: a huge bushy black beard with
grey streaks and gold rings braided into it, a black eye patch over the left eye, a big hooked nose, thick angry
eyebrows. A long crimson coat with gold trim and big gold buttons, open over a white ruffled shirt, a wide black
belt with a huge gold skull buckle, black baggy trousers tucked into tall black boots, a black tricorn hat with
gold trim and a small gold crown pinned to its front.
```
```
Big Sal (base: man, a very big round belly), about 45: the big friendly brute. Bald with a red-and-white spotted
bandana, a black goatee, a gold tooth in a wide grin, a big nose, tattoo-like painted anchors on the forearms
(simple shapes). A blue-and-white striped sleeveless shirt stretched over the belly, brown knee-length trousers
held up with a rope belt, barefoot.
```
```
One-Eyed Olga (base: woman, a little stronger arms), about 38: the tough one. Long dark red hair in a thick braid
over one shoulder, a black eye patch, a scar line across one eyebrow, a confident smirk. A red bandana on the
head, a loose white shirt with the sleeves rolled up, a brown leather waistcoat, dark green trousers, brown
knee-high boots, a wide belt with an empty holster.
```
```
Pirate Pete, the marksman (base: man, thinner), about 35: thin and twitchy. A long thin face, a pencil
moustache, narrow eyes, long greasy black hair to the shoulders. A black tricorn hat with a red feather, a long
dark-blue coat with brass buttons, a grey shirt, a red sash, black trousers, black boots, an empty holster on the
hip.
```
```
Salty Bill, the quartermaster (base: man, a little shorter), about 65: the old one. A white bushy beard and big
white eyebrows, small round spectacles on the nose, a sun-wrinkled face, a pipe-less friendly grin. A knitted
dark-green beanie, a striped blue-and-white shirt under a patched brown waistcoat with a pocket watch chain, grey
trousers rolled up at the ankles, scuffed brown boots.
```
