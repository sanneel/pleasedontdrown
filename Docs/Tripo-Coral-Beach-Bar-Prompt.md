# Tripo prompt: Coral Beach Club

Use a screenshot of the hotel as the style reference.

Create a large tropical beachfront resort bar as a standalone 3D game asset, approximately 26 metres wide, 16 metres deep and 7 metres tall.

Match the reference hotel's playful stylized architecture: warm ivory, turquoise, honey-coloured timber and subtle brass accents. Clean low-poly shapes, crisp edges and broad readable details. Tropical luxury with a relaxed beach-club atmosphere. No photorealism, dirt or weathering.

Architecture: an open-sided pavilion with a wide pitched thatched roof, generous overhangs, thick timber posts, visible rafters and a flat wooden deck. The customer-facing front is the long side. Keep spacious walking routes through the front and both sides.

Interior: a long U-shaped bar counter with an ivory base, turquoise trim and polished dark-wood countertop, roughly 1.3 metres high. Leave a clear staff workspace behind the counter, with an opening at the rear. Include a bottle-display wall with simple shelves, nine evenly spaced bar stools, two small lounge seating areas at the sides, and warm hanging lanterns. Keep the centre aisle open. Furniture must be human-sized.

Make the building attractive from every angle, especially when viewed from the sea. Use actual geometry for the counter, roof, posts and furniture. Avoid floating objects, distorted bottles, melted edges and painted-on structural features.

Model only the bar pavilion, deck, counter, shelves, seating and lamps. No people, hotel, terrain, ocean, pools or surrounding vegetation. No lettering or logos. Flat foundation and a centred ground-level pivot. Keep repeated details simple and efficient for a co-op game.
